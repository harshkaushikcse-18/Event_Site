About the University

Central University of Haryana is a Central University established under the Central Universities Act, 2009 of the Parliament. It is one of the 15 Central Universities established by MHRD, GoI under the XIth Five Year Plan (2007-12). The University is fully funded by the UGC. The university is awarded A grade by NAAC. Central University of Haryana is one of the renowned central universities in India offering various undergraduate programs in Engineering & postgraduate programs under multiple streams including Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Computer Applications, Library Science, etc. Apart from these, it also offers research courses in several specializations under Arts, Science and engineering.

About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering started in the year 2016 under the umbrella of the School of Engineering & Technology with the aim to impart high quality and practical education to the students in the field of Computer Science and Engineering. The department is currently offering a B. Tech., M.Tech. and PhD programme in CSE. The department has a highly qualified faculty members who are working on the thrust areas like WSN, Cloud Computing, Network Security, Computer Vision and Multimedia, IoT and Health Informatics, Machine Learning and Data Mining etc.

Key Objectives & Expected Outcomes

  • Train researchers and academicians in basic technologies of 5G, IoT, and Machine Learning.
  • Deliver practical sessions on IoT, Raspberry Pi, Arduino with Data Analytics and Machine Learning integration.
  • Provide cavernous understanding of IoT algorithms for researchers and practitioners.
  • Integrate advanced networks in research and development activities at higher education levels.

Tentative Contents to be Covered

  • Introduction to 5G, IoT, and Machine Learning
  • IoT and Mobile Applications
  • WSN 802.15.4 (Zigbee) & 802.15.1 (Bluetooth) Standards
  • IoT Implementation using simulator/kit (Arduino)
  • Applications of IoT in Rural Development
  • Machine Learning Techniques for Big Data Analytics
  • Hands-on and Demo on Healthcare sensor using IoT and Machine Learning
  • Big Data Analytics on Cloud & 6G and Beyond

Targeted Participants & Downloads

  • Faculty Members & Research Scholars
  • Software Developers & Network Engineers
  • Data Analysts & Young Engineers
  • Anyone interested in learning IoT concepts and applications.
