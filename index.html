01 — About

About the University

Central University of Haryana is a Central University established under the Central Universities Act, 2009 of the Parliament — one of 15 Central Universities set up by the MHRD, GoI under the XIth Five Year Plan (2007–12). Fully funded by the UGC and awarded 'A' Grade by NAAC, CUH offers undergraduate programs in Engineering and postgraduate programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Computer Applications and Library Science, alongside research programmes in Arts, Science and Engineering.

About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering started in 2016 under the School of Engineering & Technology, aiming to impart high-quality, practical education in CSE. The department currently offers B.Tech, M.Tech and PhD programmes, with faculty working across WSN, Cloud Computing, Network Security, Computer Vision & Multimedia, IoT & Health Informatics, and Machine Learning & Data Mining.

Objectives

  • Train researchers and academicians in the basic technologies of 5G, IoT and Machine Learning.
  • Deliver practical sessions on IoT, Raspberry Pi and Arduino integrated with Data Analytics and ML approaches.
  • Present a deep understanding of IoT algorithms for researchers and practitioners.
  • Integrate advanced networks into research & development activities at master's/doctorate level.

Expected Outcome

The seminar focuses on addressing key aspects with hands-on implementation:

  • 5G Technology
  • Internet of Things
  • Machine Learning
  • Network Data Analytics

Attendees will leave technically competent in the basic concepts of 5G, IoT and Machine Learning, with exposure to open-source tools for building and evaluating predictors.
